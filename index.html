On-Page SEO

Keyword mapping, meta titles, headings, and internal linking — every page optimized to rank.

What this covers

  • Keyword mapping
  • Meta titles
  • Headings
  • Internal linking

About this service

Every page on your website should have one clear job: matching what people search for and showing Google exactly what the page is about. On-page SEO does that by mapping the right keywords to the right pages, writing meta titles that describe each page properly, and structuring headings so the content is easy for search engines and visitors to follow. Internal linking then connects your pages, so authority flows through the site and visitors can find what they need. The result is a website where each page has a purpose, no pages compete with each other, and Google understands your content far better.

How it works

  2. 2Plan. Keywords are mapped to the pages they belong on, so every page has a clear target.
  3. 3Implementation. Meta titles, headings, and internal links are updated page by page.
  4. 4Report. You get a summary of what was changed across your pages.

Who this is for

About this service

Technical SEO fixes the machinery underneath your website: how search engines crawl it, which pages get indexed, and how the site is structured.
